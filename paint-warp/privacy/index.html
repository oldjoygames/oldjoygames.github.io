Paint Warp privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

PortuguêsEnglish

This policy explains which data is used when you play Paint Warp on Android. Paint Warp is developed by Old Joy Games ("we").

Data stored by the game

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, sound setting and whether you own Premium) is stored only on your device. We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email or other personal data. We have no servers that receive game data.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions, under Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data in ads.

Where required by law, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalization in your Android settings.

Purchases

The optional Premium purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment details; the game only checks with Google Play whether your account owns Premium.

Children

We do not collect personal data from any player, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about this policy, please contact us.

Your rights

Since we do not keep personal data about you, there is no data of ours to correct or delete. For data used in ads, you can exercise your rights with Google through the links above. Uninstalling the game removes all progress saved on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
